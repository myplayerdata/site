No opinions here

We don’t rank your player. The numbers do.

We don’t decide how many goals a player scores, or who he scored them against. We record what happened in every game and apply the same math to every player. Nobody is scouted, picked or voted on.

Same math for everyone

Every point is weighted by the rating of the team it was scored against, with one formula for every player. No votes, no scouts, no favorites.

What members get

The same view a scout or advisor would build by hand, already done for every player.

Adjusted scoring

Points against a top-five team count for more than points against a team ranked fiftieth. Every game is weighted by the opponent's rating, so players on hard schedules get proper credit.

Full game logs

Each player's line for every game: ice time, goals, assists, power-play points, plus/minus, with the opponent's rating and rank beside it.

Involvement

The share of his team's goals a player had a hand in, and how he compares with his own teammates. It separates the players who drive a team from the ones riding along.

Power play and ice time

Power-play points, power-play minutes and time on ice per game, so you can see how a player is used, not only what he scored.

Goalies

Goals against per game, adjusted for the opponents each goalie actually faced, with the average rating of those opponents.

Team ratings

Every roster links to that team's rating, rank and schedule strength, updated as results come in.
